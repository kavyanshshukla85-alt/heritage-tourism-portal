Top Heritage Sites

Popular Heritage Destinations

Use the search bar to find your next cultural landmark or explore these iconic sites across India.

Hawa Mahal — Jaipur, Rajasthan

Hawa Mahal, built in 1799, is known for its ornate windows and unique architecture that allowed cool air to pass through.

Konark Sun Temple — Odisha

Built in the 13th century, Konark is designed like a giant chariot and is renowned for its stone carvings.

Qutub Minar — New Delhi

Qutub Minar is the tallest brick minaret in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Taj Mahal — Agra, Uttar Pradesh

The Taj Mahal, built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal, is famous for its white marble architecture.
